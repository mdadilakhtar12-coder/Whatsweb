Privacy Policy - AIFM Education

Last updated: October 2026

AIFM Education ("we", "us") helps students and parents with information about our courses and admissions. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect
- Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, the course you are interested in and your city, when you fill a form on our website, landing page or Facebook / Instagram / Google advertisement, or when you contact us.
- WhatsApp conversations: the messages you send to our WhatsApp number and the messages we send to you, including whether they were delivered and read.
- Call records: the date, time and length of calls between you and our counsellors.

2. How we use it
- To answer your enquiry and tell you about courses, fees, batches and admissions.
- To send you WhatsApp messages, calls, SMS or emails about your enquiry and to follow up if you have not replied.
- To improve our service, for example to see which advertisement or form brought an enquiry.
We do not sell your information.

3. Who can see it
- Our own counsellors and staff, only to help you with your enquiry.
- Service providers that run our systems for us: WhatsApp / Meta (to send and receive messages) and our cloud database provider (to store the information safely).
- Authorities, only if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it
We keep your details while your enquiry or admission is active and for a reasonable time after, so that we can help you if you come back. After that we delete or anonymise them.

5. Your choices
- To stop WhatsApp messages from us, reply STOP to any message. You will not receive further messages. Reply START to receive them again.
- You can ask us to show, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

6. How to delete your data
Send us a WhatsApp message on +91 96921 45626 with the words "Delete my data" from the phone number you used with us. We will delete your details from our records and confirm to you on WhatsApp, normally within 7 days.

7. Children
Our courses are for students; we speak to parents or guardians when the student is a minor, and we ask only for the details needed for the enquiry.

8. Changes
We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
